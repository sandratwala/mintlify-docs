## From Battery Testing to Reuse

Probe connects physical battery testing with a digital platform for managing battery data, inventory, and reuse.

|             🔋            |            📡           |            ⚙️           |                     🔋📦                    |
| :-----------------------: | :---------------------: | :---------------------: | :----------------------------------------: |
|    **Battery Testing**    |    **IoT Telemetry**    |     **Backend API**     |           **Inventory & Booking**          |
| Measure battery condition | Capture sensor readings | Process and manage data | Connect batteries with reuse opportunities |

---

## How Probe Works

**🔋 Battery** → **📡 IoT Hardware** → **⚙️ Backend** → **🔋📦 Inventory** → **🔄 Reuse**

A battery is tested using Probe's connected hardware. Measurements are transmitted to the backend, processed and stored, and then made available through the platform for inventory management and reuse.
